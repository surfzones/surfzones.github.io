Surf Indicator

A simple long-term TradingView indicator that marks the exact buy and sell zones on the chart.

  • BuyBlue marks below the candles when price is at or below the 200-week SMA and monthly RSI is at or below 50.
  • SellOrange marks above the candles when price is at or above 2× the 200-week SMA and monthly RSI is at or above 75.
  • Carry-overIf a month closes in a zone, the next month opens in it, and the signal holds for that whole month even if price leaves.

Checked, didn’t qualify

A coin needs at least two buy cycles, a cycle that reached the sell zone or doubled, and to still be a liquid top-100 coin.

  • BNBOnly one full cycle so far (bought 2023, sold 2024–25). Needs at least two.
  • SOLOne buy cycle so far (2026) and it never reached the sell zone.
  • TRXOnly one full cycle so far (bought 2022, sold 2024–25). Needs at least two.
  • ZECOnly one full cycle so far (bought 2020–21, sold 2025–26). Needs at least two.
  • ADAOne buy cycle so far (2022–23) and it never reached the sell zone.
  • LINKOne buy cycle so far (2022–23) and it never reached the sell zone.
  • AVAXOne buy cycle so far (2024–25) and it never reached the sell zone.
  • DOTOne buy cycle so far (2024–25) and it never reached the sell zone.
  • BCHOne buy cycle so far (2021–22) and it never reached the sell zone.
  • NEAROne buy cycle so far (2024–25) and it never reached the sell zone.
  • DASHBought in 2 cycles but never reached a profitable sell zone, and each buy window came in lower than the last.

Newer coins like HYPE, LEO, SUI, CC, TON, TAO, SHIB, ENA, ONDO, PUMP don’t have 200 weeks of history yet, so the system can’t be tested on them.

Not every coin respects the cycle. Don’t see your coin? It hasn’t earned a spot yet.
